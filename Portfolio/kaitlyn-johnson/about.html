About Me!

I'm Kaitlyn Johnson, a writer, educator, and advocate for the transformative power of literature.

How I Got Here

I grew up in a small Arizona town where higher education wasn't the norm—where only 17% of adults held college degrees and pursuing one was often discouraged. But a handful of community college professors saw something in me and fought alongside me for my education. That experience shaped everything that followed: a deep belief that everyone deserves access to learning, and that literature has the power to change lives.

My passion for making literature accessible took root at my local public library, where I noticed the classical fiction section gathering dust and youth programs dwindling. As a library assistant, I advocated for adding canonical works to our collection and took over programming. Within months, our children's Storytime grew from three attendees to over fifteen each week. I launched TEEN, STEM, and LEGO Clubs that are still running today. One of my proudest moments was watching a teenager check out a classical novel I'd recommended for purchase—proof that when we make great literature available, people reach for it.

My Prospected Academic Goals

My goal is simple: to become the kind of English professor who advocates for students the way mine advocated for me, to create spaces where everyone feels confident in their ability to communicate, and to keep great literature in rotation and accessible to all. I believe that every individual has their own way of communicating, their own story to tell, and their own contribution to make to the larger global conversation.
